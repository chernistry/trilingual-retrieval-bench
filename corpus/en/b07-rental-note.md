## Decision

Your application for rental assistance was reviewed on 5 June 2025. The unit finds that you are **eligible** for the assistance period described below, subject to the conditions in this note.

## Calculation

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Monthly rent in lease | 5,400 ₪ |
| Ceiling for a household of two in this region | 4,800 ₪ |
| Rent counted for assistance | 4,800 ₪ |
| Household share of the counted rent | 40 percent |
| Monthly assistance | 2,880 ₪ |

The assistance is calculated on the ceiling, not on the actual rent. The 600 ₪ difference between the rent and the ceiling remains the tenant's responsibility.

## Period

Assistance runs from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026, twelve months in total, so the maximum total is 34,560 ₪. Payment is made on the 10th of each month directly to the tenant's registered account. In the first two months, a single combined payment of 5,760 ₪ will be paid on 10 August 2025.

## Conditions

1. The lease must be a written agreement of at least twelve months. The agreement on file was signed on 28 May 2025 and covers 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026.
2. The apartment may not be rented from a first-degree relative.
3. A change of address requires a new lease and written notice within 14 days.
4. The tenant must submit a signed declaration of income every six months, the first by 31 December 2025.
5. If monthly household income after three months of work exceeds 11,800 ₪, the percentage is reduced to 25.

## What the note does not cover

This note does not include property tax discounts, utility subsidies or the deposit. A one-time deposit grant of up to 7,200 ₪ is handled under a separate application, file RA-D-2025-30276, which has not yet been decided.

## Appeals

You may submit a written objection within 30 days of the date of this note, that is, by 9 July 2025. Send it to the unit's address or email, quoting the file number. The appeals committee meets on the second Wednesday of each month.
