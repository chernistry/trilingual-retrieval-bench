The surveyor from our office inspected the bathroom on 9 January. The cause is a failed extractor fan. We will fit a new fan and then repaint the ceiling with anti-mould paint. Work will take two days, 3 and 4 February 2025. The bathroom will be usable in the evening of each day. Cost: 1,450 ₪, paid by the landlord.

These repairs do not change your monthly rent of 4,900 ₪. No part of your security deposit of 9,800 ₪ will be used for them. Your rent payment for February is due on 5 February as usual.

If a pipe bursts or you smell gas, do not wait for the scheduled date. Call the emergency number listed on the notice board in the lobby and then inform our office the same day.
