1. A valuation of the property by a surveyor approved by the bank, showing a value not lower than the assumed price.
2. Proof of equity of at least 760,000 ₪ in your accounts on the date of signing.

5. No new debts above 15,000 ₪ before signing.
6. A clear title: the property must be free of liens, apart from the bank's own.
