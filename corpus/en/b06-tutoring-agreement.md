## 1. Subject and goal

The tutor will give private lessons in mathematics at the 4-unit level in preparation for the school year tests and the matriculation exam in the summer of 2027. The first goal is to raise the student's term grade from 62 to at least 75 by the end of the school year in June 2026.

## 2. Schedule

Lessons take place every Monday and Wednesday from 17:00 to 18:00 at the tutor's home. Online lessons by video are possible when the student is ill or the roads are closed. A missed lesson may be made up within two weeks if the tutor is told at least 12 hours in advance.

The agreement starts on 9 February 2026 and runs until 30 June 2026.

## 3. Fees and payment

1. The fee is **160 NIS** per lesson.
2. Each month is billed on the last day for the lessons held, and payment is due within seven days.
3. A package of ten lessons paid in advance costs 1,450 NIS, a saving of 150 NIS.
4. Lessons cancelled with less than 12 hours' notice are charged at 50 percent, which is 80 NIS.
5. Payment by bank transfer to the tutor's account or by a payment app. A receipt is sent by email within three days.

## 4. Materials

The tutor provides practice sheets free of charge, up to 40 pages a month. The student brings her notebook, calculator and the school textbook. Printed mock exams are charged at 12 NIS each.

## 5. Progress reports

At the end of each month the tutor sends the parent a short written summary: topics covered, test results, and recommended homework. A review meeting by message is held on 30 April 2026.

## 6. Ending the agreement

Either side may end the agreement with two weeks' written notice. Lessons already held remain payable. If the tutor ends the agreement, prepaid unused lessons are refunded within ten days.

## 7. Privacy

The tutor keeps the student's grades and exercises private and deletes them within 60 days after the agreement ends.
