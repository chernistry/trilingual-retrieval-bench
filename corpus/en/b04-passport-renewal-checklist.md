## What to bring

1. Your current passport, even if it is expired.
2. Your identity card.
3. One printed photograph, 5 x 5 cm, white background, taken in the last six months. Photographs taken at the office cost 32 ₪ extra.
4. The payment receipt, if you paid online.
5. If your name changed, the marriage certificate or court order.
6. If you are replacing a lost passport, a police report dated within 30 days.

## Rules for the photograph

- Face fully visible, neutral expression, both eyes open.
- No glasses with tinted lenses and no head covering unless worn for religious reasons, with the face clear.
- No filters or edited images.

## After the visit

Biometric data is taken at the counter, so you must attend in person. The passport is ready within 12 working days and is sent by registered mail to the address above. If you chose express delivery, it arrives within 4 working days. The old passport is cancelled and returned to you with a punched hole.

## Travel note

Many countries require that a passport be valid for at least six months from the date of entry. If you plan a trip before 31 October 2025, ask about the urgent track at the counter.

## Changes and cancellation

To change the appointment, contact the office at least 24 hours in advance. This confirmation does not guarantee that the passport will be issued; the office may request more documents.
