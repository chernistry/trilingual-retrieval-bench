## 6. Cancellation

- More than 180 days before the event: the deposit is forfeited.
- 90 to 180 days before: 40% of the total price is payable.
- Fewer than 90 days: 75% is payable.
- Fewer than 14 days: 100% is payable.

If the venue cannot hold the event for reasons within its control, it will refund all sums paid within 30 days.

## 7. Weather

## 8. Noise and music
