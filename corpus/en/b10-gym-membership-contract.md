## Freezing the membership

A member may freeze the plan for up to 60 days in total per contract year, with at least 7 days' written notice. Longer freezing is allowed with a doctor's note for illness or injury, or with army reserve service documents. A frozen month costs 29 ₪.

## Cancellation

1. During the first 14 days after signing, the member may cancel by written notice and receive a refund of fees paid, less the registration fee and fees for days used.
2. After the minimum term the member may cancel at any time with 30 days' written notice sent to members@harborpeak.example or delivered to the front desk.
3. Before the end of the minimum term, early cancellation is possible only on relocation more than 30 km away, medical grounds with a certificate, or conscription. Otherwise the remaining fees stay payable, up to a maximum of 5 months.
4. The club confirms each cancellation in writing within 7 days.

## Price changes

The monthly fee may change once per contract year with 45 days' written notice. A member who disagrees may cancel without penalty before the change takes effect.

## Rules

- Lockers may be used only during the visit and are emptied nightly.
- The club is not responsible for items left unattended.
- The club may suspend a member for unsafe behaviour or non-payment lasting more than 30 days.
