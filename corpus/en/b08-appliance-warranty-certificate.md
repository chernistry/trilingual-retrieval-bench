## Warranty period

The warranty is **24 months** from the purchase date and ends on **8 October 2027**. The drum motor is covered for **5 years**, until 8 October 2030, for the motor and its control board only.

## What is covered

1. Defects in materials or workmanship under normal household use.
2. Labour and replacement parts at no charge.
3. A technician visit to the owner's home during the first 12 months. In the second year, a home visit costs 120 ₪ unless the fault is confirmed as a manufacturing defect.

## What is not covered

- Damage from incorrect installation, power surges or water pressure outside the range in the manual
- Damage caused by foreign objects left in pockets, such as coins or wires
- Scale deposits from untreated hard water
- Cosmetic damage, including scratches and dents after delivery
- Consumables: door gasket wear, hoses and filters, except in the first 6 months
- Repairs by a person not authorised by the importer

## How to request service

1. Call 03-000-0000 on Sunday to Thursday between 08:00 and 17:00, or open a request on the importer's website.
2. Have the serial number, the invoice and this certificate ready.
3. A technician will be scheduled within 5 working days. For a fault that causes water leakage, the target is 48 hours.
4. After a repair, you will receive a signed service report. Keep it with this certificate.

## Replacement

If the same part fails three times within the warranty period, or if the machine is out of use for more than 30 days in total because of repairs, the importer will replace it with the same or an equivalent model or refund the purchase price.
