## Conditions of use

1. The permit allows parking in the marked residents' bays of Zone C, between 17:00 and 08:00 on weekdays and all day on Saturdays and public holidays.
2. During business hours (08:00 to 17:00, Sunday to Friday) the permit allows up to 3 hours in blue-and-white bays only. Longer stays require a pay-by-app ticket.
3. The permit is linked to one vehicle. If you change vehicles, you must update the registration within 14 days. The change fee is 35 ₪.
4. The permit is electronic. Wardens check the plate against the register, so no sticker is needed in the windscreen.
5. Guests may be given a temporary pass of up to 3 days, up to 6 times a year, free of charge, through the municipal app.
