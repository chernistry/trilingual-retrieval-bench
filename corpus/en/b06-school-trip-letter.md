# Letter to Parents: Grade 4 Trip to the Negev

**Ofek Elementary School**
14 Herzl Street, Beer Sheva
Phone: 08-000-0000 | Email: office@ofek-school.example

Date: 9 November 2025

Dear parents and guardians of Grade 4,

We are pleased to invite your child to the annual grade 4 trip to the Ramon Crater area. The trip is part of the geography and science programme and is led by the school's nature teacher, Tamar Golan, together with the class teachers.

## Date and schedule

- **Trip date:** Thursday, 4 December 2025
- **Departure:** 07:15 from the school gate on Herzl Street
- **Return:** about 16:30 to the same place
- **Route:** visitor centre, short guided walk along the crater rim, geology workshop, picnic lunch, and a stop at the ibex viewpoint

Please be at the gate by 07:00. The buses will not wait for late arrivals.

## Cost

The cost per child is **145 NIS**, which covers two buses, the guide, workshop materials and entrance fees. The school parents' committee has set aside a fund so that no child misses the trip because of cost. If you need help, please write to the class teacher in confidence before 20 November.

Payment is made through the school's payment page or in cash to the secretary on Sunday or Tuesday mornings, between 08:00 and 09:30. Please quote the reference TR-2025-118. The deadline is **Thursday, 20 November 2025**. Payments after that date can be accepted only if places remain.

## Permission form

Return the signed form at the bottom of this letter to the class teacher by 20 November. Children without a signed form cannot join the trip and will stay in a parallel class at school.

## What to bring

1. A hat and a water bottle holding at least 1.5 litres
2. Sturdy closed shoes
3. A packed lunch without nuts; the school cannot provide meals
4. A light jacket, since mornings in the desert are cold in December
5. Any regular medication, in a labelled bag handed to the accompanying teacher

Phones may be brought but must stay in the bag during activities.

## Health and safety

Two trained first aiders and a paramedic student will travel with the group. Parents who wish to volunteer as additional escorts should email trips@ofek-school.example by 14 November. Volunteers must carry an identity document and complete a short safety briefing on 2 December.

If the weather is dangerous, the trip will move to Thursday, 11 December 2025. We will confirm by message to the parents' group by 18:00 on the evening before.

Kind regards,

Hila Ben-David
Principal, Ofek Elementary School
