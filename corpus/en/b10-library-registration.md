## Borrowing rules

1. Up to 10 items per adult card and 5 items per child card at one time.
2. The loan period is 21 days for books and 7 days for DVDs and board games.
3. Each loan can be renewed twice, online or at the desk, unless another member has reserved the item.
4. The late fee is 0.50 ₪ per item per day, up to a cap of 15 ₪ per item.
5. Lost or damaged items are charged at replacement cost plus a 10 ₪ handling fee.
6. Borrowing is blocked when unpaid fees exceed 30 ₪.
