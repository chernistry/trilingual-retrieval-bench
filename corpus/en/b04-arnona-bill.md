## Early payment and late payment

If the whole annual amount is paid by 31 January 2026, a prepayment discount of 2 percent applies, which reduces the total to 6,056 ₪. Overdue amounts carry interest and linkage from the due date. A reminder is sent after 30 days. After 90 days the council may begin collection proceedings.

## Discounts and objections

Residents may apply for a reduction if they are new immigrants, pensioners, people with a disability rating, or low-income households. The application form is on the council website. An objection to the area or tariff zone must be filed in writing within 90 days of this bill, by 5 April 2026, with a measured floor plan.
